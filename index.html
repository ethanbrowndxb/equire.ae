Verification

Verified before it goes live.

Every business passes four checks before it receives the Verified badge.

  • 01Trade licenceConfirms the business is registered and the seller owns it.
  • 02FinancialsRevenue and profit are taken from the actual accounts.
  • 03LeaseWe confirm the premises and the remaining lease term.
  • 04Site visitOur team visits in person, photographs the business and confirms it is trading.
