The problem

Buying or selling a UAE business still runs on word of mouth.

Property search moved online and earned people’s trust years ago. Business sales did not. Deals still move through private contact books, forwarded PDFs and classifieds that nobody checks.

Opaque

No single place to look

Businesses change hands through personal networks and scattered classifieds. Nobody can see what is actually for sale.

Unverified

Trust comes too late

Buyers cannot check a trade licence, real turnover or lease status until they have signed an NDA and spent hours with a seller.

Unsafe

Fake and inflated listings

Duplicate, stale and inflated listings crowd out the real ones and waste everyone’s time, brokers’ most of all.

Outdated

Directories, not products

Existing sites feel like 2010 classifieds, a long way from the map-and-filter search buyers already use for property.

How a deal moves

Every listing follows one flow.

The same four steps on every listing, whether it is a café in JLT or a logistics group in Sharjah.

  1. Discover

    Search verified listings by sector, emirate, price and turnover. Every card shows photos, a financial snapshot and its verified status.

  2. Verify

    Sign the NDA in the app with DocuSign to unlock full financials, contracts and the lease in a secure data room.

  3. Connect

    Message the owner or their broker directly. Every conversation stays in one thread, attached to the listing.

  4. Close

    Agree terms and move to the share purchase agreement, MoA addendum and licence amendment, with each step tracked.

Verification

Nothing goes live until we have checked it.

Every listing passes the same checks before it earns the verified badge. Buyers see what was checked and when, so trust is built into the listing from the start.

01 · TRADE LICENCE

The business exists, and the seller owns it

The licence is matched against the issuing authority, whether DET in Dubai or the relevant free zone.

  • Licence number, activity and expiry
  • Mainland or free zone jurisdiction
  • Owners and partners named on the MoA
02 · FINANCIALS

The numbers on the card are real

Turnover and profit come from the uploaded statements, not from the asking price the seller would like.

  • Last two years of accounts
  • Structured into turnover, profit and margin
  • Flagged where figures do not reconcile
03 · LEASE

The premises come with the deal

For many sectors the lease is the business, so we check it before any buyer falls in love with the location.

  • Tenancy contract and Ejari
  • Years remaining and rent
  • Landlord NOC needed for transfer
NDA-gated data rooms

Buyers sign a DocuSign NDA in the app to unlock full financials, contracts and the lease. Owners and brokers see exactly who opened what.

Sector playbooks

Every sector sells differently, so equire guides each path.

What buyers check, and which approvals the sale needs, change by sector. Listings carry the right fields and the right checklist for each.

Mainland sales run share purchase agreement, then notarised MoA addendum, then DET licence amendment, typically in 1 to 2 weeks (4 to 6 with regulator approvals). Free zone transfers are handled in-house by the authority, often in days.
